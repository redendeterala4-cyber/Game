Number Trail

Link neighbouring number tiles so they add up to the target. Longer trails score more!

How to play
  1. Tap tiles that touch each other (side, up, down or diagonal) to build a trail.
  2. When your trail adds up to the target, tap SUBMIT. The tiles vanish and new ones drop in.
  3. Gold tiles double your points. Clear ⚡ storm tiles before their countdown reaches 0, or you lose a heart.
  4. Every 250 points you level up and win back a heart. Lose all 3 hearts and the game ends.
